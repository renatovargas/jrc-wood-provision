# Proposals to refine the spatial explicitness of forest available for wood supply

Context: FAWS 2020 (100 m, EU27, one year, likely binary) is the baseline. TR3 spatialised timber flow with DMP over CLC forest (1 km). Priority combines usefulness for the update and ease. "Verified online" = the landing page or catalogue record was reached during this research (Oct 2026). Everything else is **unverified** (from prior knowledge; check before use). Any large layer must be reduced in R (`terra`) to national or country-level summaries or to a coarse raster (<50 MB) before entering `data/`.

## Suggested order

1 and 2 (same family as FAWS, no new data pipeline), then 6 (the only option that changes the allocation weights per region), then 3 and 4 for time variation, then 5, 7 and 8 as sensitivity checks.

## Cautions

- FAWS is a 2020 snapshot. For other years, FAWS cannot be asserted; keep the share FAWS/forest of 2020 per pixel as an assumption and flag it in the document.
- FAWS reproduces national statistics only approximately (87% vs 89% area; p.98 of JRC132358): conserve the national total by normalising, not by trusting the map area.
- Do not mention contract or payment information in the documents.
